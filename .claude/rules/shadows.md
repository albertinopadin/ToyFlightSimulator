# Shadows

### Shadows (Shadows/, Display/Protocols/ShadowRendering.swift, Shadow.metal, Lighting.metal)
4-cascade cascaded shadow maps. `ShadowCascadeFitting` splits the view frustum with the uniform/logarithmic hybrid (λ = 0.5), fits each slice with a rotation-invariant bounding sphere (radius depends only on FOV/aspect/slice depth, not camera rotation) and snaps the light-space origin to world-space texel multiples — together these kill shimmer as the camera moves. Straight-overhead sun is handled by building the light basis directly with an X-axis up-vector fallback instead of `Transform.look` (the old NaN-matrix bug). `ShadowCamera` wraps per-cascade view-projection + depth range; ortho Z padding is additive to bound casters when the depth range straddles 0.

Snap phase and the floating origin: along each of the light's X and Y axes (`ShadowCascadeFitting.lightBasis`, used by both the fit and the phase update), the texel grid of cascade i is the set of positions `phase_i + k * texelSize_i` for whole k. `fitCascades` takes the phases as `cascadeSnapPhases` (`[double2]`, meters; the default, empty, means zero, a grid through the current origin) and returns the texel sizes it used in `CascadeFit.texelSizes`. Each directional `LightObject` stores both (`_cascadeSnapPhases`, `_cascadeTexelSizes`). `FloatingOrigin.applyOffset` (called by `SceneManager.updateFloatingOrigin`) calls `shiftShadowSnapPhases(by:)` with the offset it subtracts from the nodes, which sets `phase = positiveRemainder(phase - dot(lightAxis, offset), texelSize)` (`snapPhasesAfterOriginShift`). Without it the grid stays tied to the origin while the world moves by an offset that is not a whole number of texels, and every static shadow edge jumps by up to half a texel on the shift. The phases are Double so a chain of shifts adds no Float rounding, and they are positions in the current frame, so they stay smaller than one texel however far the flight goes. `fitCascades` also accepts a phase outside `0 ..< texelSize`. The pure functions are covered by `ShadowCascadeFittingTests` (one shift, 200 shifts in a row, each with a control that leaves the phase update out); the phase update through a fitted `LightObject` (`shiftShadowSnapPhases`, reached from `FloatingOrigin.applyOffset`) needs Metal and is covered by the app-hosted `LightObjectSnapPhaseTests`: one shift, two shifts in a row (the second update must build on the first), and one pass through `FloatingOrigin.applyOffset` with a real unparented `Camera`, each with a control that leaves a phase update out. Those tests pass the camera in (the fit takes it as a parameter, next paragraph), so they never read `CameraManager.CurrentCamera`. The texel helpers both files use (`shadowMapTexelCoordinate`, `subTexelDistance`) are in `TestSupport/ShadowTexelSupport.swift`.

Where the fit runs: `GameScene.update()` calls `LightObject.shadowCameraSnapshot(of:)` once on the current camera and then `fitShadowCascades(to:)` on every directional light in `LightManager`, after `super.update()` has traversed the scene and before `SceneManager.updateFloatingOrigin`. `LightObject.update()` only copies type, model matrix, position, direction and slack into `lightData`. The fit used to be in `LightObject.update()`, in the middle of the traversal. A parented camera receives its parent's world matrix only when the parent updates, so a sun that came before the aircraft in the scene's direct children (the order after a runtime aircraft swap, which appends the new aircraft) fitted to last tick's camera: one tick of flight behind on ordinary ticks (4.17 m at 250 m/s and 60 Hz) and about 1 km off on the tick after a floating-origin shift. The order fit, then phase shift, within one tick matters: on a shift tick the fit pairs the camera and the phases from before the shift, and the next tick's fit pairs the shifted camera with the shifted phases. `LightObjectCascadeFitTests` (app-hosted) covers the two functions with snapshots it builds itself; the call in `GameScene.update()` reads the live managers and has no automated test.

Shadow map storage: one `depth32Float` `texture2DArray`, 4096² × 4 slices. `ShadowRendering` encodes one render pass per cascade, binding that cascade's VP at buffer index 13 (`TFSBufferIndexShadowCascadeVP`); no `setDepthBias` — bias is slope-scaled in-shader from `shadowWorldSlack`. Light space is forward-Z ortho (clear 1.0, `.less*`) even though the main camera is reverse-Z. Sampling (Lighting.metal): `SelectCascade` by view-space depth → 5×5 hardware `sample_compare` PCF → cross-fade to the next cascade over the last 10% of each cascade's range (`CASCADE_BLEND_FRACTION = 0.1`); out-of-bounds projection falls through to the next cascade (texel-snap edge case).
